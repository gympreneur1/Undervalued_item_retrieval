title: "TradeHunter Quickstart: First Search in Five Minutes"
sidebarTitle: "Quickstart"
description: "Set up your TradeHunter account, connect your first data source, configure your deal criteria, and run your first scored deal search in under five minutes."
---

This guide walks you through everything you need to go from zero to your first actionable deal report. By the end, you'll have a live TradeHunter account, at least one data source connected, your search criteria configured, and results flowing into your Google Sheets workbook — all in under five minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Your Account">
    Navigate to [app.tradehunter.app](https://app.tradehunter.app) and click **Get Started Free**.

    You have two sign-up options:

    - **Email & Password** — Enter your email, create a strong password, and verify your address via the confirmation email sent to your inbox.
    - **Google SSO** — Click **Continue with Google** to authenticate instantly using your existing Google account. No separate password required.

    After signing in for the first time, you'll be prompted to select your business type (Broker, Investor, Reseller, or Both) and enter your primary market region. These settings pre-configure your dashboard and deal-scoring model to match your use case from the start.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose Your Module">
    TradeHunter offers two deal-finding modules. You can activate one or both depending on your business.

    | Module | Best For |
    |---|---|
    | **Commercial Real Estate** | Agents, brokers, and investors seeking undervalued properties, ROI analysis, tax incentive mapping, and highway-visibility land parcels |
    | **Resale & Auction Finder** | Resellers and flippers aggregating below-market inventory across 20+ auction and marketplace platforms |

    Select your module(s) on the onboarding screen and click **Continue**. You can toggle modules on or off at any time from your **Account Settings → Modules** page.

    <Tip>
      You can run both the Commercial Real Estate and Resale & Auction Finder modules simultaneously. Many TradeHunter users operate across both verticals and benefit from a unified daily report covering properties and resale inventory in a single Google Sheets workbook.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect a Data Source">
    TradeHunter pulls live data from the platforms you authorize. Connecting a source takes about 60 seconds.

    **For Commercial Real Estate:**
    1. Go to **Settings → Integrations → CoStar**.
    2. Enter your CoStar API credentials (username and API key). If you don't have a CoStar account, click **Request Access** to be directed to CoStar's signup page.
    3. Click **Test Connection** to confirm the link is active, then click **Save**.

    **For Resale & Auction Finder:**
    1. Go to **Settings → Integrations → Marketplaces**.
    2. Toggle on any platforms you want to monitor. Supported platforms include Facebook Marketplace, GovDeals, AuctionZip, Proxibid, HiBid, PublicSurplus, eBay, Craigslist, and 12 more.
    3. For platforms that require authentication (e.g., Facebook Marketplace), click **Authorize** and complete the OAuth flow in the pop-up window.
    4. Click **Save Sources** when finished.

    Your enabled sources are saved to your profile and used automatically in every subsequent search.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set Your Search Criteria">
    Search criteria tell TradeHunter what a deal looks like to you. Saved criteria run automatically on every daily scan.

    **For Commercial Real Estate:**
    - **Property Types** — Select from Retail, Office, Industrial, Land, Mixed-Use, Multi-Family, and more.
    - **Area Codes** — Enter one or more NC area codes (e.g., `336` for Greensboro/Winston-Salem/High Point, `919` for Raleigh, `704` for Charlotte) to define your geographic focus.
    - **Max Purchase Price** — Set a ceiling to filter out listings above your budget.
    - **Minimum Discount vs. FMV** — Enter a percentage (e.g., `15%`) so TradeHunter only surfaces properties priced at least that far below their estimated fair market value.
    - **Highway Visibility** — Toggle on to prioritize land parcels with high-traffic road frontage, particularly along major corridors in the 336 market.

    **For Resale & Auction Finder:**
    - **Categories** — Choose from Electronics, Furniture, Industrial Equipment, Fitness Equipment (including Vintage), Vehicles, Apparel, and more.
    - **Keywords** — Add specific keywords (e.g., `Nautilus`, `Universal Gym`, `Cybex`, `vintage weight machine`) to narrow results to the inventory you know best.
    - **Platforms** — Select which of your connected platforms to include in this specific search profile.
    - **Max Listing Price** — Set a price ceiling to filter results to your working budget.
    - **Minimum Deal Score** — TradeHunter scores every listing from 0–100. Set a floor (e.g., `70`) to see only high-confidence deals.

    Click **Save Search Profile** to name and store your criteria. You can create multiple profiles (e.g., "NC 336 Land" and "Vintage Fitness Equipment") and run them all in parallel.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Run Your First Search">
    With your data sources connected and at least one search profile saved, you're ready to find deals.

    1. From your dashboard, click **Find Deals** next to any saved search profile — or click **Run All** to execute every profile simultaneously.
    2. TradeHunter queries your connected platforms in real time, scores every matching listing, and returns ranked results within seconds to a few minutes depending on source volume.
    3. Review results in the **Deal Feed** view. Each listing shows the source platform, listing price, estimated fair market value, discount percentage, deal score, and a direct link to the original listing.
    4. Click any listing to open the **Deal Detail** panel, where you'll find additional data including property history (CRE), comparable sales, ROI projections, tax incentive flags, and seller contact information where available.
    5. Use the **Save Deal** button to pin high-interest listings to your watchlist for follow-up.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Export to Google Sheets">
    TradeHunter can push your entire deal feed — including all scored listings, metadata, and direct links — into a Google Sheets workbook automatically, every day.

    1. Go to **Settings → Integrations → Google Sheets**.
    2. Click **Connect Google Account** and authorize TradeHunter via the Google OAuth prompt.
    3. Choose whether to create a **New Workbook** (TradeHunter will provision and name it for you) or link to an **Existing Sheet** (paste in the sheet URL).
    4. Select which search profiles to include in the export and choose your preferred sheet layout: one tab per profile, or all results in a single consolidated tab.
    5. Set your **Sync Schedule** — daily at a specific time, or on-demand via the **Export Now** button in your dashboard.
    6. Click **Save & Enable Sync**.

    Starting the next morning (or immediately if you click **Export Now**), your Google Sheets workbook will be populated with a fresh, fully formatted deal report — new rows highlighted in green, expired listings automatically greyed out.
  </Step>
</Steps>
