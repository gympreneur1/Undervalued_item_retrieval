TradeHunter aggregates deals from more than 20 auction and marketplace platforms into a single, scored deal feed. Sources span traditional auction houses (GovDeals, HiBid, Proxibid), online marketplaces (Facebook Marketplace, eBay, EstateSales.net), government and university surplus programs, and specialized categories including foreclosures, tax liens, and business bankruptcy liquidations. Some platforms require an account to access full listing details, bid history, or saved-search features; others expose their full inventory publicly and are scraped automatically with no credentials needed. This page walks you through connecting each type of source, customizing which platforms appear in your searches, and configuring the bid alerts and geographic filters that keep your deal feed focused.

<Tip>
  Enable **all available platforms** when you first set up TradeHunter — even ones outside your primary category focus. TradeHunter's Deal Score and category filters will surface only the most relevant results, but a broader data pool means you are less likely to miss a cross-category gem. You can narrow or disable platforms at any time.
</Tip>

## Connecting Platforms That Require an Account

For platforms that gate listing details, bid history, or watchlist features behind a login, you connect once and TradeHunter handles all subsequent requests authenticated as you.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Auction Platform Settings">
    From your dashboard, go to **Settings → Integrations → Auction Platforms**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add Platform">
    Click the **+ Add Platform** button in the upper-right corner of the Auction Platforms panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Your Platform">
    Browse the platform list or type a name in the search field. Click the platform card you want to add.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate">
    - **OAuth platforms** (eBay, Facebook Marketplace, Google Shopping): You will be redirected to the platform's own login page. Sign in with your existing account and click **Allow** on the permissions screen. You are then returned to TradeHunter automatically.
    - **Credential platforms** (GovDeals account, HiBid, Proxibid, PropertyRoom, AuctionZip, MaxSold, and others): Enter your account email and password in the TradeHunter credential form, then click **Connect**. Credentials are encrypted and stored in your account vault.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the Connection">
    A green **Connected** badge appears on the platform card once the test request succeeds. The platform is immediately included in your deal searches.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### OAuth Platforms

**Facebook Marketplace (Meta Login)**
TradeHunter requests access to your Marketplace listings feed only. It does not access your personal profile, posts, friends list, or Messenger. During the Meta OAuth prompt, you can review and restrict the exact permissions you grant.

**eBay (eBay OAuth)**
TradeHunter requests read-only access to eBay search and listing data on your behalf. Your buyer account details, purchase history, and payment information are never accessed. Connecting your eBay account unlocks personalized saved-search syncing and ending-soon alerts that are not available through eBay's public API alone.

**Google (For Google Shopping Alerts)**
Connecting your Google account enables TradeHunter to monitor Google Shopping results for items matching your saved searches. TradeHunter requests only the Shopping-related read scope — it does not access Gmail, Google Drive, or any other Google service through this OAuth connection.

## Platforms That Are Publicly Scraped

The following platforms expose their full listing inventory without requiring a login. TradeHunter enables these sources by default for all accounts — no credentials or setup is needed.

- **GovDeals public listings** — actively listed surplus items from participating government agencies
- **PublicSurplus** — surplus auctions from local and state government entities
- **County surplus portals** — TradeHunter monitors a curated list of 200+ county and municipal surplus pages, updated weekly
- **EstateSales.net** — upcoming and in-progress estate sales by region
- **Craigslist** — for-sale listings by metro area; coverage is configured using your geographic scope settings (see below)
- **University surplus pages** — lab equipment, IT hardware, vehicles, and furniture from 80+ university surplus programs
- **Tax lien and tax deed auctions** — county-published tax certificate auction notices across all 50 states, organized by auction date and state statute
- **Business bankruptcy asset sales** — Chapter 7 and Chapter 11 notices from federal court filings, including equipment lots, commercial fixtures, and real property

You can disable any of these public sources at any time from **Settings → Integrations → Auction Platforms** by toggling the platform off.

## Platform-Level Settings

### Enabling and Disabling Individual Platforms

Every connected and public platform has an **Active** toggle in the Auction Platforms settings panel. When you disable a platform, it is excluded from all searches and deal feeds immediately — your credentials are retained, so re-enabling is instant.

### Setting Bid Alerts

For platforms where TradeHunter has account-level access, you can receive real-time notifications when:

- An item you are watching receives a **new bid**
- An auction you are watching is **ending within a chosen time window** (options: 1 hour, 3 hours, 6 hours, 12 hours, 24 hours)
- An item you are watching has its **reserve met** for the first time

To configure bid alerts, go to **Settings → Integrations → Auction Platforms → Bid Alert Settings**. Choose your preferred channels (email, in-app notification, or SMS if enabled on your plan) and set your default ending-soon threshold. You can override the threshold per-item from the deal detail page.

### Geographic Scope

Most auction platforms carry nationwide inventory. If your sourcing strategy focuses on a specific region, you can restrict platform coverage to reduce noise in your deal feed.

To set geographic scope:

1. Go to **Settings → Integrations → Auction Platforms → Geographic Filters**.
2. Select a platform from the list.
3. Choose **State**, **Metro Area**, or **Radius (miles from ZIP code)** as your filter type, then configure the value.
4. Click **Save Filter**.

Geographic filters apply at the platform level, so you can configure eBay to show nationwide results while restricting Craigslist to a specific metro. Filters for publicly scraped platforms (such as Craigslist) are required — TradeHunter will prompt you to select at least one region during initial setup.
