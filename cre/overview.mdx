TradeHunter's Commercial Real Estate (CRE) module gives investors, brokers, and developers a single command center for finding, scoring, and acting on commercial property opportunities across the United States. By pulling live data from CoStar, CREN, LoopNet, Crexi, BizBuySell, and local MLS feeds simultaneously, TradeHunter eliminates the manual work of cross-referencing multiple platforms and delivers a unified view of every listing — complete with fair market value estimates, ROI projections, tax incentive flags, and highway visibility scores. Whether you are hunting for a discounted warehouse along I-40 in the Greensboro metro or scoping out Opportunity Zone parcels in Winston-Salem, the CRE module surfaces the deals that match your criteria before the competition finds them.

## Connected Data Sources

<Note>
  TradeHunter aggregates commercial property data from the following platforms. Listing availability varies by market; coverage is most comprehensive in metros with active CoStar and CREN subscriptions.
</Note>

| Source | Data Type | Refresh Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| **CoStar** | Listings, comps, lease rates, vacancy data | Every 4 hours |
| **CREN** | Regional broker listings, off-market leads | Daily |
| **LoopNet** | National commercial listings, property history | Every 4 hours |
| **Crexi** | Active listings, auction properties, bid history | Every 4 hours |
| **BizBuySell** | Business-attached real estate, owner-occupied sales | Daily |
| **Local MLS Feeds** | Residential-zoned commercial, mixed-use, land | Daily |

<Note>
  TradeHunter does not replace a direct CoStar or LoopNet subscription for deep research workflows. The CRE module is optimized for deal discovery and comparative scoring across sources — always verify listing details directly with the listing broker before submitting an offer.
</Note>

## Getting Started

If you are new to the CRE module, the recommended workflow is:

1. **Run a property search** filtered to your target geography and property type to build a baseline list of available inventory.
2. **Apply a discount filter** (≥10–15% below FMV) to isolate undervalued opportunities.
3. **Open the ROI panel** on any property that looks promising to model your return assumptions.
4. **Check the Tax Incentive panel** to see whether federal or state programs apply — this can meaningfully change your effective cost basis.
5. **Enable the Highway Visibility filter** if site exposure along a major corridor is important for your intended use.
6. **Save your search** to receive alerts as new matching listings come to market.

Start with [Property Search](/cre/property-search) to run your first query.
