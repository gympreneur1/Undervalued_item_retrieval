## Get Up and Running

New to TradeHunter? Follow these four steps to go from sign-up to your first automated deal report.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Your Account">
    Visit [app.tradehunter.app](https://app.tradehunter.app) and sign up with your email address or use Google Single Sign-On for instant access. Choose your business type — Broker, Investor, Reseller, or All of the Above — so TradeHunter can tailor your default dashboard and deal-scoring weights from day one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Your Data Sources">
    Link the platforms that power your deal flow. For Commercial Real Estate, connect your CoStar credentials to pull live MLS and off-market data. For Resale & Auction, enable any combination of Facebook Marketplace, GovDeals, AuctionZip, Proxibid, and 16 additional supported platforms with a single OAuth toggle.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Your Search Criteria">
    Define exactly what a great deal looks like to you. Set property types, target area codes, price ceilings, minimum discount thresholds versus fair market value, and keyword filters. TradeHunter saves your criteria as reusable search profiles so you can run multiple deal strategies in parallel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive Your First Daily Report">
    Once your criteria are saved, TradeHunter begins scanning immediately. By the next morning — or within minutes if you trigger a manual search — you'll receive a scored, ranked deal report delivered to your inbox and auto-populated into your connected Google Sheets workbook.
  </Step>
</Steps>
