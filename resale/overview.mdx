## How It Works

Each day, TradeHunter's crawlers scan every connected platform for new and updated listings. Each listing is matched against a live database of comparable sold items to estimate fair market value. TradeHunter then calculates a Deal Score from 0–100 based on the discount depth, resale demand signals, platform reliability, and any available condition notes. You set your minimum Deal Score threshold, apply keyword and category filters, and TradeHunter delivers only the listings that meet your criteria — directly to your dashboard and, optionally, to a Google Sheets spreadsheet on your chosen daily schedule.

## Source Categories TradeHunter Monitors

TradeHunter aggregates listings from more than 20 platforms across seven distinct source categories:

- **Government & Surplus Auctions** — GovDeals, GovPlanet, PublicSurplus, IronPlanet, and local city and county surplus portals
- **University Surplus** — Duke Surplus, UNC Surplus, NC State Surplus, and general university asset disposal programs
- **Estate Sales & Liquidations** — EstateSales.net, GSOL (Greater Southeast Online Liquidations), MaxSold, and Caring Transitions
- **Foreclosures & Tax Liens** — county sheriff sales, Auction.com, RealtyBid, and tax deed auctions
- **Business Bankruptcies & Lab Equipment** — BidSpotter, Hilco Industrial, Aaron's Auction, LabX, and BioSurplus
- **General Marketplaces** — Facebook Marketplace, Craigslist, eBay (Buy It Now and Auction), and OfferUp
- **Specialty Auction Platforms** — HiBid, AuctionZip, Proxibid, PropertyRoom, and LiveAuctioneers

Together, these sources give you comprehensive coverage of the secondary market — from a single piece of vintage gym equipment listed on a county surplus portal to a full commercial gym liquidation running through a national bankruptcy auctioneer.
